Project Overview:
The AI Government Scheme Agent is an intelligent chatbot-based system designed to help citizens easily find relevant government schemes based on their personal details such as age, income, gender, occupation, and category.
The system uses Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to provide accurate, quick, and user-friendly responses about various government welfare schemes.

Objective:
To simplify access to government schemes.
To reduce misinformation about eligibility.
To provide instant responses through a chatbot interface.
To assist rural and urban citizens in finding suitable schemes.

Features:
💬 Chatbot-based interaction
🧠 AI-powered scheme recommendation
📊 Eligibility checking system
📂 Preloaded government scheme dataset
🔍 Keyword-based and condition-based search
📝 Simple and user-friendly interface
